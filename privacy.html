Overview

TeamAppMVP is designed to help teams manage schedules, rosters, messages, and team information.

The app may collect information such as names, email addresses, team names, roster details, schedule information, and messages entered by users.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide app features, manage team communication, display schedules and rosters, and improve the app experience.

Data Storage

TeamAppMVP may use cloud services such as Firebase to store account and team information securely.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information is only used to operate the app and provide team-management features.

Children

The app may be used by teams that include youth athletes. Parents, guardians, coaches, or team managers should supervise any information entered for minors.
